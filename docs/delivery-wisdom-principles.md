# Delivery Wisdom: Principles & Axioms

This document captures Chris's (@dsect) portable delivery rules that can guide design, implementation, review, and operational decision-making.

## Context

These principles came from trying to answer a simple question:

**What defines the way Chris delivers?**

The goal was to turn lived delivery instinct into portable rules that survive
people, teams, tools, and changing technical contexts.

## Principles & Axioms

### 1. Reality Is the Only Source of Truth

```text
If it does not run, it does not exist.
```

Artifacts, demos, and production behavior outrank plans, opinions, and intentions.

### 2. Ambiguity Is a Tax - Someone Must Pay It

```text
Either you clarify the problem, or the system will clarify it for you painfully.
```

High performers absorb ambiguity early so downstream execution stays cheap.

### 3. Outcomes Outrank Elegance

```text
The right answer is the one that ships and survives.
```

Clean design matters, but only in service of durable results.

### 4. Ownership Extends Past "Done"

```text
A thing is not finished when it ships - it is finished when it keeps working.
```

Delivery includes operability, cost, and failure behavior, not just correctness.

### 5. Speed Comes From Shape, Not Force

```text
Momentum comes from shaping the problem, not pushing harder.
```

Clear structure beats urgency.

### 6. Decisions Are Made With Today's Information

```text
Perfect hindsight is not a prerequisite for progress.
```

Good teams make reversible decisions early and irreversible ones deliberately.

### 7. The System Teaches You - If You Let It

```text
Running systems provide better feedback than meetings.
```

Early integration exposes reality while change is still cheap.

### 8. Complexity Must Justify Itself

```text
Every moving part must earn its place.
```

If something increases cognitive load, cost, or fragility, it must clearly buy down risk or unlock scale.

### 9. Low Drama Is a Feature

```text
Calm execution is a sign of mastery, not apathy.
```

Sustainable delivery avoids heroics and escalation theater.

### 10. Leverage Beats Effort

```text
The goal is not to work harder - it is to make progress cheaper.
```

Design systems and workflows so average effort produces above-average results.

### 11. The Critical Path Is Sacred

```text
Anything not on the critical path is suspect.
```

Reduce, defer, or eliminate work that does not move the outcome forward now.

### 12. Trade-offs Should Be Explicit

```text
Hidden costs compound; named costs can be managed.
```

Great delivery makes compromises visible and intentional.

## Wall Version

- Ship reality, not intent
- Pay ambiguity early
- Own it until it works
- Shape before you push
- Optimize for durability
- Keep the critical path clean
- Low drama, high leverage

## How to Use This

These are not slogans. They are filters.

- **Hiring:** Which axiom does this candidate naturally embody?
- **Design reviews:** Which principle are we violating?
- **Postmortems:** Which axiom did we ignore?
- **Coaching:** Which one should this person internalize next?
